0.2.0-rc1
- GM chooses one eligible ledger officer; other clients cannot perform concurrent independent balance edits.
- Isolate balances, treasury, history, raids and settings per guild; preserve the original saved ledger during migration.
- Bind snapshots to guild identity and guard delayed transfers against guild or permission changes.
- Add transfer bounds, consistent chunk validation and stale transfer cleanup.
- Release candidate: live multi-client acceptance testing remains required.

0.1.17-beta
- Clarify Admin fields, action labels, gold calculations, manual links, backups and guild-rule controls.
- Add DKP change examples and distinguish guest tracking from account linking and administrator permissions.

0.1.16-beta
- Guild Master can choose Blizzard officers, Remove Member permission, or named guild ranks for addon administration.
- Permission policies are guild scoped and accepted only directly from the current Guild Master.

0.1.0-beta
----------
- First public beta under the GDKP Credit name.
- Persistent person-based DKP balances.
- Dynamic Gold/DKP based on usable treasury and outstanding DKP.
- Guild Credit rounded down to whole gold.
- Manual raid-start and raid-end workflow.
- Manual World Buff + Consume readiness check.
- Default thresholds: 3+ world buffs and 2+ tracked consumes.
- Upgrade-only readiness rechecks: a prior pass cannot be lost after death or buff loss.
- Full-attendance tracking worth 4 DKP.
- World Buff and Consume checks worth 3 DKP each.
- 200 DKP cap.
- GDKP pot and 10% guild-cut tracking.
- Character-to-player mapping and alias support.
- Officer/member synchronization through WoW addon messages.
- Member DKP, Gold/DKP, Guild Credit, raid status, and history views.
- Full snapshot and raid export tools.
- Automatic migration from the original GPCDKP beta SavedVariables.
- Test automated CurseForge packaging.
